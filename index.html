Gear Terminology

Module (m)
Tooth size in mm. Module (m) = Pitch Diameter (d) / Number of Teeth (N). Two gears must share the same module to mesh.
Pitch circle
The imaginary rolling circle the tooth size is defined against — not a physical edge on the gear.
Pressure angle (α)
The angle of the tooth flank at the pitch circle. 20° is standard; it sets how "pointed" vs "stubby" the teeth are.
Backlash
A small amount shaved off each tooth's thickness so two mating gears turn freely instead of binding.
